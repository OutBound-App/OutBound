# OutBound

OutBound is a group travel planning app designed to keep trip itineraries organized in one place. Group members can pitch activities, vote on what makes the cut, and follow a shared schedule throughout their trip.

## Features

### Trip management
- Group dashboard showing destination, dates, trip details, and active members.
- Host controls to update details, cancel trips, or make manual schedule changes without a vote.
- Member invites by username or email, with options to accept or decline.

### Activity proposals and voting
- Staging area where members suggest activities with a title, time, location, and price.
- Group voting to approve activities and add them directly to the trip calendar.
- Voting to remove events if plans change.

### Itinerary and tools
- Calendar showing confirmed events, locations, scheduled times, and booking notes.
- Expense tracker to log shared costs, note who paid, and see who owes what.
- Search and category filters for food, transit, lodging, and specific days.
- Interactive map showing daily stops and route order.
